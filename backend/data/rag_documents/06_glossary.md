# Glossary

ampere capacity   The contracted supply limit for a subscriber, in amperes.
                  Determines the fixed monthly fee. Common tiers: 5, 10, 15, 20.
bypass            Part of a customer's wiring connected before the meter, so
                  that consumption is not recorded.
direct hook       A wire attached to the distribution line ahead of any meter.
                  The energy never passes a measuring device.
feeder            A distribution line serving one neighbourhood from a generator.
ishtirak          A private generator subscription service.
load factor       Average load divided by peak load. Higher is more efficient.
non-technical loss  Energy lost to theft, tampering or unrecorded connections.
OCR confidence    How certain the meter-reading recognition was, from 0 to 1.
                  Low values indicate a damaged display or a poor photograph.
phase imbalance   Unequal current across L1, L2 and L3. Causes neutral current
                  and extra heating.
power factor      Ratio of useful power to apparent power. Lower means more
                  current for the same work.
rollover          A mechanical meter passing its maximum reading and returning
                  to zero. Produces an apparently negative consumption.
technical loss    Energy converted to heat in cables and connections.
unexplained loss  The portion of total loss remaining after technical loss,
                  measurement error and unbilled connections are accounted for.
                  This is a candidate for investigation, NOT a proven theft.
