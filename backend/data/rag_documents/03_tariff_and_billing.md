# Tariff and Billing Rules

The Ministry of Energy and Water publishes a per-kWh rate each month,
derived from the average diesel price per 20-litre can and the prevailing
exchange rate. The rate is not stable: month-on-month changes of 20 to 30
percent have occurred.

Every bill has two parts:
  1. A fixed monthly fee determined by the subscriber's ampere capacity.
  2. A variable charge: kWh consumed multiplied by the month's rate.

Two rates are published each month. Subscribers in rural areas or above 700
metres altitude pay a surcharged rate, roughly 10 percent higher than the
base rate applied in urban areas below 700 metres.

Amounts are recorded in LBP. Because the underlying cost is dollarised,
every invoice stores the tariff row and the exchange rate that were used, so
a bill can be recomputed and defended months later.

Partial payment is normal, not an error. A single invoice may have several
payments recorded against it.
